The browser build uses these open-source dependencies:

- [Spark](https://github.com/sparkjsdev/spark), MIT License.
- [Three.js](https://github.com/mrdoob/three.js), MIT License.

Dependency versions are pinned in `package-lock.json`; their license texts and
copyright notices are available from the installed packages and upstream
repositories.

The included Garden from Wall scene was supplied by its creator for this demo.
It is distributed as standard `.splat` scene data, separately from the software
license. The public repository does not contain the source capture decoder or
Luma website assets.
